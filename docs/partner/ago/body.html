Proposal for the AGO

High School Credit for Private Lessons

A new benefit for AGO members who teach teenagers.

The idea

AGO members who teach piano or organ can offer their teenage students official high school Fine Arts credit. The teacher keeps teaching as usual, and Optio handles the rest.

0.5
Fine Arts credit per semester. A full year earns 1.0 credit.
$100
Per student per semester, paid by the family. Free for teachers and the AGO.

What earns the credit

  • A semester of regular private lessons with an AGO member
  • A short weekly record in the app: a photo, a practice video, or a few sentences
  • One performance: a recital, a church service, or a recorded piece

How it works

  1. The teacher signs up and gets an enrollment link.
  2. The family enrolls through the link: consent, payment, and the student's school in one form.
  3. Lessons continue as usual. The student records each week in the app.
  4. The teacher confirms at the end of the semester. It takes two minutes.
  5. Optio awards the credit and sends the transcript to the school.

Who does what

Teacher

Teaches, shares the link, and confirms each semester. No grading.

Optio

Enrollment, payment, the app, teacher review, and transcripts.

AGO

Offers it as a member benefit and tells members about it.
